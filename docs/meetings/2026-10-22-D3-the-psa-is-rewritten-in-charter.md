# D3 — The Protocol of Sovereign Agency is rewritten in Charter; Lean remains only for what the language cannot say, and that remainder shrinks

**Laws:** goal 1; 8, 13 (docs/mission.md).
**Date:** 2026-10-22
**Status:** ruled (Eric); sharpened by D10

## Ruling
The PSA becomes a `.charter` program using the language's individuals, `Int` facts, time, and in-file scenarios and invariants. Lean is kept only for what the language cannot yet say. D10 fixes what happens to that remainder: it is deleted when its Charter replacement passes, never retained.

## Alternatives weighed
Freeze the Lean PSA as a regression oracle (rejected: two spellings, Law 13). Delete it immediately (rejected: nothing to check the rewrite against until the evaluator exists).

## Reopens if
Never; D10 governs the residue.
