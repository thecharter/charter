# D43 — Federated Action-Carried Justification Bundles, Audience-Controlled Gossip, Redacted Attestations, and Partial-View Obligation Safety

**Laws:** 1, 2, 3, 6, 7, 9, 10, 11, 13, 14 (`docs/mission.md`). Brief `B30` (`2026-11-10-briefs-for-ruling.md`).
**Date:** 2026-11-10
**Status:** ruled as predicted (Eric, 2026-11-10)

## Context

Esterhuyse, Müller, and van Binsbergen's *JustAct* framework (*Logical Methods in Computer Science*, 22:3, Sept. 2026) separates slow-moving synchronized policy agreements (`agreed`) from peer-to-peer signed policy statements (`stated`) carried inside self-contained action payloads (`payload(a)`), proving that auditing an action's permission inside a closed payload remains monotone as network history grows (`Theorem 3.20`). At the same time, *JustAct* leaves six explicit limitations open: (1) policy evaluation (`eval`) and soundness (`eval-sound`) are axiomatized as unproved Rocq parameters (`Parameter 4.10`, `Axiom 4.11`) alongside a Rust evaluator with a 30,000-step bailout (§4.5.2–4.5.3); (2) revoking a previously signed statement requires wiping global `agreed` (`agreed := []`), halting every agent (§6.3.5); (3) policies do not govern statement forwarding, so leaking a sensitive peer statement remains "well-behaved" (§7.2.3); (4) prospective actors and public observers see raw private facts inside `payload(a)` (§7.2.4); (5) combinatorial search for a justifying payload is left to external solvers (§7.2.1); and (6) dynamic obligations and deadlines are omitted because partial views could otherwise punish agents for obligations beyond their knowledge (§7.2.2).

## Ruling (`D43`, `018.2`, `018.5`)

1. **Zero duplicate surface syntax (`Law 13`):** No new `.charter` keywords are introduced. Actor-scoped open/input facts, `Evidenced` + `Confidential To` propositions (`D22`), Schnorr per-actor signatures (`#76`), and `Due within d After ev` notice-gated deadlines (`009.4`, `D38`) already express the institutional rules.
2. **Operator-blind verified bundle layer (`src/Charter/Server/JustAct.lean`):** Built directly over `Charter.Eval` (`M.evalActTri` / `semPhrase`, proved sound and complete against `Charter.Core.Meaning` in `D41`) with zero `Surface/`, `Resolve/`, or `Prove/` dependencies (`test/gates/operator-blind.sh`), providing:
   - **Authorship reflection (`reflectAuthor_no_impersonation`):** A Schnorr-verified `SignedStatement` authored by `α` may only supply open/input facts whose leading actor component equals `α` or tender `Evidenced` items with `issuer = α`, never impersonating another actor or overriding closed constitutional rules.
   - **End-to-end monotone auditability (`persist_permitted_bundle`):** Evaluates Charter's three answers (`occurred`, `lawful`, `valid`, `Law 2`) plus enumerated `effectsOf` capability tokens on the closed bundle state and proves without custom axioms that any valid, unrevoked bundle remains identically valid across growing network histories (`net₁.stated ⊆ net₂.stated`, `net₁.enacted ⊆ net₂.enacted`), even when adversarial peers publish conflicting statements.
   - **Granular statement revocation (`revocation_invalidates_bundle`, `unrelated_revocation_preserves_bundle`):** Author-signed revocations (`revokedIds`) and `expiresAt` bounds invalidate targeted mandates without freezing `agreed` for unrelated actors.
   - **Audience-controlled communication (`public_gossip_always_lawful`, `restricted_leak_detected_and_unusable`):** `.public` statements gossip freely with zero reasoning overhead; forwarding `.restricted` statements outside their permitted audience (absent an author disclosure grant) still occurs (`Law 1`), records an explicit `CommunicationViolation` (`Law 2`, `Law 3`), and bars unauthorized recipients from relying on the leaked statement in `verifyBundle`.
   - **Commitment-bound redacted attestations (`redacted_bundle_agrees_with_unredacted`):** `RedactedAttestation`s carry the SHA-256 commitment of a hidden `SignedStatement` plus an authorized auditor/enclave Schnorr signature exporting only non-confidential interface facts, preserving identical `(lawful, valid)` verdicts on non-confidential acts without exposing `Confidential` facts to counterparties or public observers.
   - **Dependency-sliced bundle synthesis (`synthesizeBundle_sound`):** Selects a minimal valid statement subset from a noisy candidate pool, discarding irrelevant or adversarial statements.
   - **Partial-view obligation safety (`unnoticed_duty_never_violated`):** Over federated `AgentViews` (`gossip`, `forget`, `ackNotice`), proves that an unacknowledged notice-gated `Duty` (`Due within d After notice`) is never violated across arbitrarily delayed or lossy network traces.
3. **CLI, server, and standard library (`src/Charter/Cli/Bundle.lean`, `src/Charter/Server/Api.lean`, `lib/federated_mandate.charter`):** Exposed via `charter bundle <sign|verify|synthesize|inspect>`, `charter-server` (`SignStatement`, `RevokeStatement`, `SynthesizeBundle`, `VerifyBundle`, `EnactBundle`), and reusable module `lib/federated_mandate.charter` (`test/lib/federated_mandate_test.charter`).

## Cost Accepted & Reopens If

- **Cost accepted:** Redacted bundle privacy relies on an authorized auditor/enclave Schnorr attestation over the SHA-256 statement commitment rather than an embedded SNARK circuit; cryptographic zero-knowledge proving of `M.eval` remains external substrate territory.
- **Reopens if:** Inter-institution federation requires peer statements to carry non-ground rule schemas rather than actor-attributed ground facts, delegations, and evidence tenders.
