# 009 — Time

**Status:** approved (Eric, 2026-11-06; Charter 1.0.0). Fixed by D7, D16, and Law 7. Implementation: compliant (`src/Charter/Core/State.lean`, `src/Charter/Eval/Properties.lean`); every statement has a passing conformance test. Calendars are out of scope for 1.0 (9.6).

**9.1 The clock.** `Current Time` is an instant. It starts at 0. The phrase `@ t.` sets it to the time `t`; `tick.` advances it by one. Both are environment input (Law 6): they are not acts, appear in no transition report, and leave no event in the history; like any phrase they recompute derived facts and violations (9.5). The clock never moves backwards: `@ t` with `t` before now is CHR106 and changes nothing. `test/spec/spec_009_1_clock.eflint`, `spec_009_1b_clock_never_backwards.eflint`

**9.2 Types (D7).** `Time` (instants) and `Duration` (differences) are distinct types over `Int`, and so are the domains `Identified by Time` and `Identified by Duration`. `Time + Duration : Time`, `Duration + Time : Time`, `Time - Duration : Time`, `Time - Time : Duration`, `Duration ± Duration : Duration`; `Time + Time` is CHR012. `Duration * Int : Duration`, `Int * Duration : Duration`, `Duration / Int : Duration` (rounding toward negative infinity, 010.2); in `*` at most one operand may be a duration, an instant is never scaled, and `Int / Duration` is CHR012 (D16). Comparisons relate two times, two durations, or two integers. A literal, or arithmetic over literals, coerces to `Time` or `Duration` by demand (including a `Time` or `Duration` component of a product type); an integer fact does not. `test/spec/spec_009_2_time_and_duration_types.eflint`, `spec_009_2b_no_time_plus_time.eflint`, `spec_009_2c_no_int_fact_as_duration.eflint`, `spec_009_2d_duration_times_int.eflint`, `spec_009_2e_no_duration_times_duration.eflint`, `spec_009_2f_no_int_over_duration.eflint`, `spec_009_2g_no_time_times_int.eflint`, `spec_009_2h_product_time_literal.eflint`

**9.3 Time is state and exact clock-threshold stamps (`Law 7`, `D38`).** Every assignment carries the time it was established; re-creating an instance that holds keeps its stamp, and a derived fact keeps its stamp while it stays derived (receiving its exact threshold instant when newly derived from a clock comparison such as `Current Time >= T` or `Held(e) For d`, `#16`). `Held(e) For d` holds when `e` holds and has held for at least the duration `d`: a condition over the stamp, never an event. `Creates e For d` stamps the created instances with the expiry `now + d`; from that time they no longer hold, with no event in the history. `Done(a)` and `?occurred a` inspect recorded valid transition occurrences; `Since(e)`, `Age(e)`, `Overdue(d)`, `a Before b`, `a Within d Of b`, and `c At ev` relate instants and event-time snapshots (`#43`, `#49`). `test/spec/spec_009_3_held_for.eflint`, `spec_009_3b_stamp_kept_on_recreate.eflint`, `spec_009_3c_derived_keeps_stamp.eflint`, `spec_009_3d_expiry.eflint`, `spec_009_4b_done_moments_within_before_and_at.charter`

**9.4 Deadlines, `Grace`, and `Every` (`D38`, `#44`, `#47`).** `Due within d` on a duty is `Violated when Held(d()) For d` (006.7); `Due within d Grace g` makes `?late` hold at `d` and `?violated` at `d + g`; `Due within d After ev` counts `d` from `Since(ev)`; and `Every p` derives numbered period obligations purely from the clock (`Law 7`) with optional `Paid by` and `On breach Creates`. `test/spec/spec_006_7_due_within.eflint`, `spec_009_5b_exact_clock_stamps_grace_and_every.charter`

**9.5 The clock is a step.** Setting the clock recomputes derived facts (a rule may read `Held`) and re-evaluates violations, so a deadline passing is noticed at the phrase that moves the clock, not by a synthetic lapse event. `test/spec/spec_009_4_clock_recomputes.eflint`

**9.6 Civil calendar (`D38`, `#48`).** ISO `YYYY-MM-DD` date literals denote exact proleptic Gregorian days since `1970-01-01` (`Domain.time`); `days` and `weeks` denote `Duration` units; `Year(t)`, `Month(t)`, `Day(t)`, and `Weekday(t)` (`1` = Monday .. `7` = Sunday) inspect civil dates; and `AddMonths(t, m)` and `AddYears(t, y)` advance calendar dates with Catala-style month-end clamping (`src/Charter/Core/Calendar.lean`). `test/spec/spec_009_6_calendar_dates_units_and_add_months.charter`

*Spellings this draft chose, for Eric:* `Held(e) For d` for the plan's "`held for d`"; `@ t.` and `tick.` as the plan writes them; `tick` is therefore a reserved word in Charter mode (not in `--eflint` mode).
