# Durable Power of Attorney for Finances

An illustrative example for the Charter language. It is not legal advice and is not written for any jurisdiction.

## P.1 Appointment

I, Helen Brooks, the principal, appoint Marcus Brooks as my agent for my finances. This power of attorney takes effect when I sign it.

## P.2 Authority

The agent may pay my bills and move money between my accounts. What the agent does within this authority binds me as if I had done it myself.

## P.3 Limits

The agent may make a gift of my money of no more than five hundred dollars. A larger gift does not bind me. The agent may not change the beneficiary of any account. An attempt by the agent to do so has no effect.

## P.4 Durability

This power of attorney is durable. The agent's authority continues while I am incapacitated.

## P.5 Revocation and death

I may revoke this power of attorney at any time while I have capacity. The agent's authority ends when I revoke it, and it ends at my death.

## P.6 Third parties

A person who deals with the agent without notice that the agent's authority has ended is protected: the dealing binds me or my estate. Once that person has notice, it does not.

## P.7 Records

The agent keeps a record of every payment, transfer and gift made for me. The agent gives me an account of them within thirty days of my request.
