# Last Will and Testament of Margaret Ellis

An illustrative example for the Charter language. It is not legal advice and is not written for any jurisdiction.

## W.1 Revocation

I revoke every will I made before this one.

## W.2 Execution

This will takes effect only if I sign it in the presence of two witnesses who each sign it too. A gift to a person who witnessed this will is void.

## W.3 Executor

I appoint Daniel Ellis as my executor. If Daniel is unable or unwilling to act, I appoint Priya Shah in his place. The executor's authority begins at my death.

## W.4 Debts

The executor pays my debts before distributing any gift.

## W.5 Gifts

I give my house to Anna Ellis. I give twenty thousand dollars to Ben Ellis.

## W.6 Survivorship

A beneficiary who does not survive me by thirty days is treated as having died before me, and that beneficiary's gift falls into the residue.

## W.7 Residue

I give the rest of my estate to the Harbor Animal Shelter.
