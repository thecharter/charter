# Bylaws of the Elm Street Garden Club

An illustrative example for the Charter language. It is not legal advice and is not written for any jurisdiction.

## Article 1. Purpose

1.1 The Elm Street Garden Club is a non-profit association. Its purpose is to keep the community garden on Elm Street.

1.2 The club pays no part of its funds to any member as profit.

## Article 2. Members and dues

2.1 A person becomes a member by paying the annual dues. The annual dues are thirty dollars.

2.2 A membership lasts one year from the payment of dues, and ends then unless the member has paid dues again.

2.3 The members may change the amount of the annual dues by a motion at a meeting.

## Article 3. Meetings

3.1 The board calls a meeting of the members at least once a year, by giving every member at least seven days' notice.

3.2 A meeting has a quorum when at least one third of the members are present. A meeting without a quorum decides nothing.

3.3 Each member present has one vote on each question. Unless these bylaws say otherwise, a motion carries when more members vote for it than against it.

## Article 4. The board

4.1 The board has three offices: president, secretary and treasurer. No member holds two offices.

4.2 At a meeting the members elect a member to each office. The candidate who receives the most votes for an office is elected.

4.3 An officer serves for one year from election.

4.4 The members may remove an officer by a vote of two thirds of the members voting.

## Article 5. Amendment

5.1 The members may amend these bylaws by a vote of two thirds of the members voting, at a meeting called with at least fourteen days' notice.

5.2 Article 1 cannot be amended, and neither can this clause.

## Article 6. Adoption

6.1 Ada Okafor, Bram Visser and Carmen Diaz, the founding members, adopt these bylaws and have each paid the first year's dues.

6.2 Until the first election Ada is president, Bram is secretary and Carmen is treasurer.
