# Warranty Deed

An illustrative example for the Charter language. It is not legal advice and is not written for any jurisdiction.

## D.1 Conveyance

Robert Hale, the grantor, conveys Lot 7 to Sofia Marin, the grantee.

## D.2 Delivery

This deed takes effect when the grantor has signed it and delivers it to the grantee.

## D.3 Title

A grantor conveys only the title the grantor holds.

## D.4 Warranty

The grantor warrants that the land is free of liens. The grantor pays off any lien that was on the land at delivery within thirty days of the grantee's demand.

## D.5 Recording

Until it is recorded, a deed does not bind a later buyer who pays for the land without notice of the deed.
