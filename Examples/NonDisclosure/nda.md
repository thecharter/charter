# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

An illustrative example for the Charter language. It is not legal advice and is not written for any jurisdiction.

## 1. Parties and purpose

1.1 Tidewater Robotics and Juniper Analytics are the parties. Each may disclose confidential information to the other while they consider a joint venture, which is the purpose of this agreement.

1.2 Confidential information is whatever a party marks as confidential when it hands it to the other. The party handing it over is the discloser and the party receiving it is the recipient.

## 2. Secrecy and use

2.1 The recipient does not disclose the discloser's confidential information to anyone else.

2.2 The recipient uses the discloser's confidential information only for the purpose.

## 3. Exceptions

3.1 Clauses 2.1 and 2.2 do not apply to information that is public, that the recipient already knew before it was handed over, or that the recipient developed independently.

3.2 The recipient may disclose confidential information that a court orders it to disclose, but only after notifying the discloser of the order.

## 4. Term

4.1 Clauses 2.1 and 2.2 bind the recipient for three years from the day the information was handed over, and not after.

## 5. Return

5.1 The recipient returns or destroys the discloser's confidential information within ten days after the discloser asks for it.
