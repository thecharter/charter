# Delegated Treasury Mandate Charter

## TM.1 Appointment and Express Mandate Bound

A company may appoint an accountant to purchase approved bonds using a specified custody account up to the stated mandate cap. Any bond purchase beyond the mandate cap is not authorized on the company's behalf except where apparent authority protects an innocent counterparty without revocation notice.

## TM.2 Reporting Duty and Non-Imputation

An unauthorized purchase is recorded as an observed external event even when it has no authorized corporate effect. The accountant must submit a trade confirmation within two days of any observed purchase; a missing report breaches the reporting duty without establishing concealment or theft by the company unless the company directed the misconduct.

## TM.3 Custody Evidence and Adjudicated Findings

Custody trade confirmation is evidenced by custodian and accountant filings; conflicting filings leave confirmation conflicted until an empowered audit panel issues a finding under the verified-receipt standard. Each purchase receipt and execution intent nonce may be recognized or settled at most once.

## TM.4 Safety and Accounting Guarantees

Recognized corporate bond holdings stay within the mandate cap unless apparent authority applied prior to revocation notice, no purchase receipt or execution intent is settled twice, and independent delegate misconduct is never imputed to the company without company direction.
