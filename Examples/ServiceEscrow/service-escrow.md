# Service Hosting and Emergency Freeze Agreement

## SE.1 Hosting Continuity and Advance Notice
A provider hosts a client workload under an active hosting agreement. The provider may suspend a hosted node after giving seven days advance notice, or immediately under a staked emergency freeze. Suspending a hosted node without advance notice or an active emergency freeze breaches the provider's continuity duty.

## SE.2 Staked Emergency Freeze and Escalation
An auditor may initiate a temporary emergency freeze for at most three days by posting an audit stake at least equal to the required stake for that auditor's prior unfounded freeze count. If the arbiter dismisses the freeze as unfounded, the freeze ends immediately, the auditor's unfounded freeze count increases by one, and the provider must restore the node within two days.

## SE.3 Account Termination Protection
The arbiter may terminate an account only when a confirmed breach remains uncured. While the client disputes termination in good faith, the account is immune from termination unless an uncured confirmed breach holds.

## SE.4 Invariants and Acceptance
No node remains under an emergency freeze after the freeze window expires without arbiter confirmation. An unfounded freeze increments the initiator's unfounded count and triggers a restoration deadline.
