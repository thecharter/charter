# Promissory Note

An illustrative example for the Charter language. It is not legal advice and is not written for any jurisdiction.

## 1. The loan

1.1 Marcus Dunn, the lender, lends ten thousand dollars to Elena Brooks, the borrower, who promises to repay it with interest as this note says.

## 2. Interest

2.1 The unpaid principal bears simple interest at six percent a year. At the end of every thirty days after the loan is made, one twelfth of a year's interest on the principal then unpaid is added to the interest owing. Unpaid interest does not itself bear interest.

## 3. Instalments

3.1 The borrower pays twelve instalments of eight hundred sixty dollars and sixty-six cents. The first falls due thirty days after the loan is made, and each of the others thirty days after the one before.

3.2 The twelfth instalment is whatever is then owing, whether that is more or less than the others.

## 4. Late fee

4.1 An instalment that is not paid in full within ten days after it falls due is late. For each late instalment the borrower owes a late fee of five percent of a regular instalment.

## 5. Application of payments

5.1 Every payment is applied first to late fees, then to interest, and then to principal.

## 6. Prepayment

6.1 The borrower may pay all or part of what is owing ahead of time. The lender charges nothing for early payment. Money paid ahead of time counts toward the instalments that fall due next.

## 7. Default

7.1 If an instalment is still unpaid thirty days after it falls due, the lender may, by notice to the borrower, declare everything owing due at once. A notice given before then has no effect.
