# Vehicle Bill of Sale

An illustrative example for the Charter language. It is not legal advice and is not written for any jurisdiction.

## 1. Sale

1.1 Grace Tanaka, the seller, sells her 2015 Corolla to Leo Varga, the buyer, for six thousand five hundred dollars.

## 2. Ownership and title

2.1 Ownership of the car passes to the buyer when the buyer has paid the price and the seller has delivered the car. A seller passes only the ownership she has.

2.2 The seller hands the signed certificate of title to the buyer within five days after ownership passes.

## 3. What the seller promises

3.1 The seller warrants that she owns the car and that there is no lien on it.

3.2 The seller states that the odometer reads 84,210 miles and that she knows of no reason why that is not the distance the car has travelled.

3.3 If the seller did not own the car, or there is a lien on it, or the seller knew the odometer reading was wrong, the buyer may cancel the sale. The car then goes back to the seller, and the seller refunds the price within ten days after the cancellation.

## 4. As is

4.1 The car is sold as is. The seller gives no warranty of its condition, and the buyer has no claim against the seller for a defect.

4.2 That does not protect a seller who knew of a defect and concealed it from the buyer. The seller pays for the repair of such a defect within thirty days after the buyer demands it.
