# Tenancy Agreement

The toy the spec and the tests use (plan §4, §8): six clauses between one landlord and one
tenant, written so that every Phase 2 addition to the language has one clause that needs it.

## T.1 Rent

The tenant pays the rent within five days of the first of each month. Rent not paid within those five days is in arrears. The amount of the rent is fixed at the start of the tenancy and changes only by review under T.6.

## T.2 Entry

The landlord may enter the premises after giving the tenant one day's notice. The landlord may also enter without notice to deal with an emergency. An entry with neither notice nor emergency is a breach.

## T.3 Quiet enjoyment

The tenant is entitled to quiet enjoyment of the premises: an entry the tenant was not given notice of has no effect on the tenant's rights, unless an emergency is in progress.

## T.4 Deposit

The landlord holds the deposit in trust for the tenant. The deposit never exceeds two months' rent. A deposit taken above that amount is void and the excess is returned.

## T.5 Repairs

The landlord repairs any defect the tenant has reported within seven days of the report. While an emergency repair of the premises is under way, the ordinary repair duty is suspended.

## T.6 Rent review

The rent may be changed only by a review the tenant was notified of thirty days in advance. A change without that notice has no effect.
