# Residential Purchase Agreement

An illustrative example for the Charter language. It is not legal advice and is not written for any jurisdiction.

## 1. Sale

1.1 Nora Whitfield, the seller, sells the house at 14 Alder Lane to Omar Reyes, the buyer, for four hundred thousand dollars.

1.2 This agreement binds the parties once both of them have signed it. The day the second of them signs is the signing day.

## 2. Earnest money

2.1 Within three days after the signing day the buyer pays earnest money of ten thousand dollars to Harbor Title, the escrow agent.

2.2 The escrow agent holds the earnest money and pays it out only as this agreement says.

2.3 If the earnest money is not paid in time, the seller may cancel this agreement.

## 3. Inspection

3.1 For ten days after the signing day the buyer may have the house inspected and may cancel this agreement for any reason. If the buyer cancels under this section, the earnest money is returned to the buyer.

## 4. Financing

4.1 This sale depends on the buyer obtaining a loan. For thirty days after the signing day the buyer may cancel this agreement if no lender has committed to make the loan. If the buyer cancels under this section, the earnest money is returned to the buyer.

## 5. Closing

5.1 Closing takes place within forty-five days after the signing day. At closing the buyer pays the seller the rest of the price and the seller delivers the deed to the buyer.

5.2 When both have been done the sale is closed: title to the house passes to the buyer, and the earnest money is paid to the seller as part of the price.

## 6. Default

6.1 If the seller does not deliver the deed in time, the buyer may cancel this agreement and the earnest money is returned to the buyer.

6.2 If the buyer does not pay the rest of the price in time, the seller may cancel this agreement and keeps the earnest money.

## 7. Risk of loss

7.1 Until closing the risk of loss stays with the seller. If the house is destroyed before closing, the buyer may cancel this agreement and the earnest money is returned to the buyer.

## 8. Cancellation

8.1 A cancellation that this agreement does not allow has no effect. Once this agreement is cancelled, neither party owes the other anything more under it.
