# Employment Agreement

An illustrative example for the Charter language. It is not legal advice and is not written for any jurisdiction.

## 1. Employment

1.1 Harborlight Bakery, the employer, employs Nadia Okafor, the employee, as head baker. Employment begins when both have signed this agreement.

1.2 Employment is at will. Either party may end it at any time, with or without cause.

1.3 The employee gives the employer fourteen days' notice before she leaves. Leaving without that notice is a breach of this agreement, but it still ends the employment.

## 2. Pay

2.1 The salary is six thousand dollars a month. The employer pays each month's salary within five days after the month ends.

2.2 The employer reimburses a business expense within thirty days after the employee submits it.

## 3. Termination and severance

3.1 The employer has cause to terminate when the employee has stolen from the employer, has been convicted of a felony, or has neglected her duties after a written warning.

3.2 If the employer terminates the employment and the employee signs a release of claims within twenty-one days after the termination, the employer pays her severance of two months' salary within ten days after she signs. A release signed later than that earns no severance.

3.3 A termination for cause carries no severance.

## 4. After employment

4.1 The employee discloses the employer's confidential information only with the employer's written consent. This clause continues to bind her after the employment ends.

4.2 For twelve months after the employment ends, the employee does not solicit any member of the employer's staff to leave the employer.

4.3 Salary and expenses that fell due before the employment ended remain payable after it.
