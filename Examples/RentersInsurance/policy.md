# Renters Insurance Policy

An illustrative example for the Charter language. It is not legal advice and is not written for any jurisdiction.

## R.1 The policy

Harbor Mutual, the insurer, insures Nora Quinn, the insured, for her personal property at Flat 4, 12 Mill Lane. This policy covers a loss that happens while it is in force.

## R.2 Premium

The insured pays a premium of twenty dollars for each month, due on the first day of that month.

## R.3 What is covered

The insurer covers the loss of the insured's personal property by theft or by fire.

## R.4 What is not covered

The insurer does not cover a loss, even one by theft or fire, if flood contributed to it, if the insured caused it on purpose, or if it is ordinary wear.

## R.5 Limit and deductible

For a covered loss the insurer pays the value of the property lost, less a deductible of five hundred dollars. The insurer never pays more than twenty thousand dollars for one loss.

## R.6 Notice and proof of loss

The insured gives the insurer notice of a loss within thirty days after it happens. The insurer owes nothing for a loss it was not given notice of within that time. After giving notice the insured sends the insurer a proof of loss stating what was lost, its value, and its cause.

## R.7 Payment

The insurer pays a covered loss within thirty days after it receives the proof of loss.

## R.8 Disputed cause

Either party may dispute a cause the other has stated for a loss. Ines Okafor, the adjuster, then decides whether it was a cause of the loss, and her finding binds both parties. When a cause is disputed, the thirty days for payment run from the adjuster's finding.

## R.9 Cancellation

If a premium is unpaid after it is due, the insurer may give the insured notice of cancellation. This policy ends ten days after that notice, unless the insured pays the overdue premium before then. The insurer may not cancel this policy for any other reason.
