# Roommate Agreement

An illustrative example for the Charter language. It is not legal advice and is not written for any jurisdiction.

## M.1 The household

Asha Verma, Luis Ortega and Mei Tanaka, the roommates, rent the flat at 9 Canal Street from Grove Lettings, the landlord.

## M.2 Rent

The rent is two thousand dollars a month. The roommates share it in proportion to the floor area of their rooms: Asha's room is fourteen square metres, Luis's twelve and Mei's ten. A share is rounded down to the cent. Asha pays the landlord the whole rent on the first of each month, and each of the others pays Asha their share by the same day.

## M.3 Joint liability

Each roommate is liable to the landlord for the whole rent, whoever has paid a share to whom.

## M.4 Utilities

A roommate who pays a utility bill is owed one third of it by each of the others, rounded down to the cent, within fourteen days after showing them the bill.

## M.5 Leaving

A roommate who wants to leave gives the others thirty days' notice, and may not move out before the thirty days have passed. A replacement takes the leaving roommate's room, share and place in this agreement only when every other roommate has approved the replacement. Until then the leaving roommate remains a roommate, liable for rent and utilities, whether or not they have moved out.

## M.6 Quiet hours

Quiet hours run from ten at night to seven in the morning. During quiet hours no roommate makes noise that can be heard in another roommate's room.

## M.7 Guests

A roommate's guest may stay at most three nights in a row. A longer stay needs the consent of every other roommate.
