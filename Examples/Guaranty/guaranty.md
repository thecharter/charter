# Guaranty

An illustrative example for the Charter language. It is not legal advice and is not written for any jurisdiction.

## 1. The guaranty

1.1 Victor Lang, the guarantor, guarantees to Ines Marlow, the lender, the loan of eight thousand dollars that the lender has made to Dana Cole, the borrower. The borrower is in default if the loan is not repaid in full within ninety days after the date of this guaranty.

1.2 While the borrower is in default the lender may demand payment from the guarantor. The guarantor pays what the lender has demanded within fifteen days after the demand.

## 2. Limit

2.1 The guarantor's liability under this guaranty is limited to five thousand dollars in all. The lender may not demand more than remains of that limit, or more than the borrower then owes, and a demand for more has no effect.

## 3. Notice of default

3.1 The lender gives the guarantor notice of the borrower's default within thirty days after the default. The lender may not demand payment unless that notice was given in time, and a demand made without it has no effect.

## 4. Changes to the loan

4.1 The lender does not change the terms of the loan without the guarantor's consent. If the lender does, the guarantor is released, and no demand made after the change has any effect.

## 5. The guarantor's claim against the borrower

5.1 A guarantor who pays the lender takes over the lender's claim against the borrower for the amount paid. The borrower then owes that amount to the guarantor and no longer owes it to the lender.
