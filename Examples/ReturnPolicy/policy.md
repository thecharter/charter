# Calloway Cycles Return and Warranty Policy

An illustrative example for the Charter language. It is not legal advice and is not written for any jurisdiction.

## 1. Returns

1.1 A customer may return an item to Calloway Cycles within thirty days after buying it.

1.2 A customer who returns an item with its receipt receives a refund of the price paid. A customer who returns an item without its receipt receives store credit for the price instead.

1.3 An item sold as final sale cannot be returned.

## 2. Warranty

2.1 If an item proves defective within one year after it was bought, the shop repairs or replaces it once the customer brings it in.

2.2 If the shop has not repaired or replaced the item within thirty days after the customer brought it in, the shop refunds the price paid.

## 3. Refunds

3.1 The shop pays a refund within ten days after it becomes due.
