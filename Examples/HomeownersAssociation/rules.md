# Rules of the Larch Court Homeowners' Association

An illustrative example for the Charter language. It is not legal advice and is not written for any jurisdiction.

## Article 1. The association

1.1 The members of the Larch Court Homeowners' Association are the owners of the six houses in Larch Court: Ada Okoye, Boris Lindqvist, Carmen Ruiz, Dev Patel, Elif Kaya and Farid Haddad.

1.2 The board manages the association and acts for it under these rules.

## Article 2. Assessments

2.1 Each owner pays the association an assessment of one hundred fifty dollars for each month, due on the first day of that month.

2.2 An assessment not paid within fifteen days after it is due bears a late fee of twenty-five dollars.

2.3 An assessment still unpaid thirty days after it is due becomes a lien on the owner's house, and the lien remains until the assessment and its late fee are paid.

## Article 3. Violations and fines

3.1 The board may fine an owner for a violation of these rules only after written notice and a hearing.

3.2 The notice states the violation alleged. The hearing is held no sooner than ten days after the notice, so that the owner can prepare an answer.

3.3 At the hearing the board finds whether the owner committed the violation.

3.4 The board may fine the owner up to one hundred dollars for a violation it has found. A fine for a violation the board has not found has no effect.

3.5 The owner pays a fine within thirty days after it is imposed.

## Article 4. Architectural changes

4.1 An owner may not change the outside of a house without the approval of the architectural committee.

4.2 The owner applies to the committee in writing. If the committee has neither approved nor refused an application thirty days after it was made, the application is deemed approved.

4.3 A refusal made after those thirty days has no effect.

## Article 5. Amendment

5.1 These rules are amended only by the votes of two thirds of the owners.

5.2 An owner whose house is under a lien has no vote.

## Schedule A. Proposed amendment
