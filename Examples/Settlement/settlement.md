# Settlement Agreement and Mutual Release

An illustrative example for the Charter language. It is not legal advice and is not written for any jurisdiction.

## 1. Parties and the incident

1.1 Elena Voss, the claimant, has sued Northgate Parking, the defendant, over the collapse of a garage gate onto her car, which this agreement calls the incident.

1.2 This agreement takes effect when both parties have signed it.

## 2. Payment

2.1 The defendant pays the claimant eighteen thousand dollars within fourteen days after this agreement takes effect.

## 3. Release

3.1 When the defendant has paid in full, each party releases the other from every claim arising from the incident. Until then no claim is released.

3.2 Neither party brings a claim that it has released.

3.3 The claimant dismisses the lawsuit within seven days after the defendant has paid in full.

## 4. Late payment

4.1 If the defendant has not paid in full within the fourteen days, the claimant may rescind this agreement. After rescission the release is treated as never having taken effect, and the claimant returns whatever the defendant has paid.

## 5. Confidentiality

5.1 Neither party discloses the terms of this agreement. A party may disclose them where the law requires it.

## 6. No admission

6.1 Nothing in this agreement is an admission of fault by either party.
