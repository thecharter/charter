# Prenuptial Agreement of Elena Vargas and Tomas Reed

An illustrative example for the Charter language. It is not legal advice and is not written for any jurisdiction.

## N.1 Signing and disclosure

Before signing, each party lists for the other the property that party owns. This agreement binds the parties once both have signed it. It is void if a party owned property at signing that the party left off the list.

## N.2 Separate property

Property a party owns when the parties marry is that party's separate property. A gift or inheritance one party receives during the marriage is that party's separate property.

## N.3 Marital property

All other property either party acquires during the marriage is marital property.

## N.4 Mixing

Separate property that a party puts into the parties' joint account becomes marital property.

## N.5 Divorce

On divorce each party keeps that party's separate property. The marital property is divided equally between the parties.

## N.6 Term

This agreement lapses when the parties have been married for ten years. It has no effect on a divorce after that.
