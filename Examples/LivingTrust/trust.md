# The Ellis Family Revocable Living Trust

An illustrative example for the Charter language. It is not legal advice and is not written for any jurisdiction.

## Article 1. Creation

1.1 Margaret Ellis, the settlor, transfers the trust fund to the trustee to hold in trust. The settlor is the first trustee.

## Article 2. Revocation and amendment

2.1 While she is alive and has capacity, the settlor may revoke this trust or name a different successor trustee.

2.2 On the settlor's death this trust becomes irrevocable.

## Article 3. Incapacity

3.1 The settlor is incapacitated when two physicians certify in writing that she cannot manage her affairs.

3.2 While the settlor is incapacitated, and after her death, the successor trustee acts as trustee. The first successor trustee is Daniel Ellis.

## Article 4. The trustee's duties

4.1 The trustee may not mix trust property with the trustee's own property.

4.2 The trustee accounts to the beneficiaries within sixty days after the end of each year.

4.3 A sale of trust property to the trustee is void unless every beneficiary consents to it.

## Article 5. Distributions

5.1 During the settlor's life the trustee pays the settlor whatever she requests from the trust fund.

5.2 At the settlor's death the trust fund is divided between Anna Ellis and Ben Ellis in equal shares.

5.3 The trustee holds a beneficiary's share until that beneficiary reaches the age of twenty-five, and then pays it to the beneficiary.
