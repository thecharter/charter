# Parenting Plan for Mia Castellan

An illustrative example for the Charter language. It is not legal advice and is not written for any jurisdiction.

## Article 1. Regular schedule

1.1 Mia lives with her parents, Rosa Castellan and Daniel Castellan, in alternating weeks. The first week is Rosa's.

1.2 On the first day of a parent's week the other parent hands Mia over.

## Article 2. Holidays

2.1 Mia spends the winter holiday with Daniel and the spring holiday with Rosa.

2.2 The holiday schedule overrides the regular schedule. While a holiday lasts, the parent who has the holiday keeps Mia even in the other parent's week.

## Article 3. Missed time

3.1 A parent who keeps Mia more than a day into the other parent's time owes the other parent make-up time. The make-up time is given within sixty days.

## Article 4. Child support

4.1 Daniel pays Rosa six hundred and fifty dollars of child support for each month.

4.2 Each payment is due by the fifth day of the month. A payment made within a grace period of three more days is not a breach.

## Article 5. Relocation

5.1 A parent who intends to move Mia's home gives the other parent sixty days' notice. A move without that notice is a breach of this plan.

## Article 6. Changes

6.1 This plan changes only by order of the family court. An agreement between the parents to change the support amount has no effect until the court orders it.
