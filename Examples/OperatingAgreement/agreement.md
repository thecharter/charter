# Operating Agreement of Tidewater Kayak LLC

An illustrative example for the Charter language. It is not legal advice and is not written for any jurisdiction.

## Article 1. Members and capital

1.1 Iris Nakamura, Jonah Pratt and Kemal Aydin are the members of Tidewater Kayak LLC, the company.

1.2 Iris has contributed $50,000, Jonah $30,000 and Kemal $20,000. No member is required to contribute more.

1.3 A member's percentage interest is that member's capital as a share of the capital of all the members.

## Article 2. Decisions

2.1 The members vote in proportion to their percentage interests.

2.2 An ordinary decision is made by members holding more than half of the percentage interests.

2.3 A decision to admit a new member or to sell the business is made only by all the members.

2.4 A decision made without the vote this agreement requires has no effect.

## Article 3. Distributions

3.1 The members may by ordinary decision distribute cash the company holds.

3.2 A distribution is divided among the members in proportion to their percentage interests. Each share is rounded down to the cent, and what is left over stays with the company.

## Article 4. Sale of an interest

4.1 A member who wants to sell an interest first offers it to the other members at the price a buyer is willing to pay.

4.2 The other members have thirty days from the offer to accept it. If both accept, they buy the interest in proportion to their percentage interests. If one accepts, that member buys all of it.

4.3 If neither accepts within the thirty days, the member may sell the interest to the buyer for no less than the offered price, once the buyer has been admitted as a member.

4.4 A sale of an interest that does not follow this article has no effect.

## Article 5. Withdrawal

5.1 A member may withdraw from the company by giving the other members ninety days' notice.

5.2 When the notice period ends, the company buys the withdrawing member's interest. The price is the member's percentage interest in the book value of the company, less ten percent.

5.3 The company pays the price within sixty days after the notice period ends.

## Article 6. Dissolution

6.1 The company is dissolved when all the members decide to dissolve it, or when only one member remains.

6.2 On dissolution the company pays its debts and then distributes what remains to the members in proportion to their percentage interests. After dissolution the company makes no other distribution.

## Article 7. Amendment

7.1 This agreement may be amended only by members holding at least three quarters of the percentage interests. An amendment made without that vote has no effect.

## Schedule A. First Amendment

A.1 Clause 5.2 is amended to read: When the notice period ends, the company buys the withdrawing member's interest. The price is the member's percentage interest in the book value of the company.
