# Advance Healthcare Directive of Walter Okafor

An illustrative example for the Charter language. It is not legal advice and is not written for any jurisdiction.

## H.1 My agent

I, Walter Okafor, appoint my daughter Grace Okafor as my healthcare agent. If Grace is unable or unwilling to act, my brother Samuel Okafor is my agent in her place. This directive takes effect when I sign it.

## H.2 When my agent decides

While I have capacity, I make my own treatment decisions. My agent decides for me only while I lack capacity.

## H.3 Capacity

I am presumed to have capacity. I lack capacity when my attending physician determines in writing that I cannot understand or communicate a treatment decision. If another physician disagrees in writing, the hospital ethics committee decides the question, and until it decides neither my decisions nor my agent's are acted on. If my attending physician withdraws the determination, I have capacity again.

## H.4 My agent's choices

While my agent decides for me, my agent may consent to or refuse any treatment, except as H.5 says.

## H.5 My instruction

If my attending physician has diagnosed me as terminally ill, I do not want life support. This instruction overrides my agent's choice: my agent's consent to life support then has no effect.

## H.6 Revocation

I may revoke this directive, or dismiss an agent, at any time, whether or not I have capacity.
